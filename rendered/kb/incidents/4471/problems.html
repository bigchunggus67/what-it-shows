The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first. Our tenants write the day first, so a booking for 7 April comes out as 4 July. Every channel shows it: app, email, and booking-v2’s form. This is the bug #4471 was opened for.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY under it. People at day-first tenants type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads the date their tenant’s way, day first. The result looks the same as the parser bug, a day and month swapped, but it only comes in through the booking-v3 form, and the parser fix does not touch it. The fix is the hint (or the form’s reading), not the parser.

one-day-move: dates move one day earlier. These reports do not swap day and month. A Tuesday shows as the Monday before; an invite arrives before the booking was made; the same meeting lands on Tuesday for a client in Lisbon and Monday for one in Toronto. That points at time zone handling, not at date order. The desk had already noted these reports as a one-day move, but the cause table filed them under parser by tenant, so they had no ticket.

Why one ticket hid them

  • Merging on subject (decision 0001) puts every “wrong date” report in one ticket, whatever its cause. The subjects of all three problems match.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. A US browser at a European tenant gets tagged hint; a European browser on the booking-v3 form gets tagged parser. Counting tags gives the wrong split. The cause comes from the tenant: its locale, its booking form and the channel.
  • The export repeats reports. The ticket system’s pages overlap, so some reports appear twice. Count by report id.

For the desk

  • Keep #4471 for the parser. Link the other two tickets from it.
  • Don’t close #4471 when the parser fix ships. Check that the booking-v3 and one-day reports have stopped too.
  • Merging on subject alone will do this again. A decision record that replaces 0001 should merge on cause (tenant, form and channel), not subject.
